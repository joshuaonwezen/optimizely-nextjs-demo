# Mosey Bank content standards

Use this skill when you write, review or audit Mosey Bank web content. Mosey Bank is a fictional
UK retail bank whose site is also published in other languages. The rules below follow the spirit
of UK financial-promotion rules: content must be **fair, clear and not misleading**. They're a demo
checklist, not legal advice.

## Voice

- Plain English, short sentences, second person ("you"). Explain any term a customer may not know.
- Confident, not salesy. No exclamation marks in headings or body copy. No "best", "cheapest",
  "number one" or "unbeatable" unless the page cites a named, dated source.
- Sentence case in headings. "Mosey Bank" in full on first mention, never "MB".
- Pages in other languages follow the same rules in natural language, not word-for-word translation.

## Financial-promotion checklist

Flag every breach. Quote the exact words, name the rule and propose a compliant rewrite.

| # | Rule | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | Never promise returns. No "guaranteed returns", "risk-free", "can't lose" or "safe as houses" for investments. | Investments, pensions, stocks and shares ISAs |
| R2 | Investment content carries a risk warning, for example: "The value of investments can go down as well as up, and you may get back less than you put in." | Investments, pensions, stocks and shares ISAs |
| R3 | Past performance is never presented as a guide to future returns without saying so. | Investments |
| R4 | Savings rates state AER and whether the rate is variable or fixed (and for how long). | Savings, cash ISAs |
| R5 | Credit (loans, cards, overdrafts, mortgages) that quotes a rate or cost also gives a representative example (APR, total amount payable) or links to one. | Loans, credit cards, overdrafts, mortgages |
| R6 | Mortgages: "Your home may be repossessed if you do not keep up repayments on your mortgage." | Mortgages |
| R7 | Deposit protection is stated accurately: eligible deposits are protected by the FSCS up to £120,000 per person. Never imply investments are FSCS-protected in the same way. | Savings, current accounts |
| R8 | Benefits and risks get equal prominence; small print does not contradict the headline. | All |
| R9 | No urgency pressure ("only today", "act now before it's gone") unless the offer really ends on a stated date. | Offers, campaigns |
| R10 | A page in another language carries that market's equivalent of the warning its English page needs. Dutch credit pages state the annual percentage rate (JKP) and "Let op! Geld lenen kost geld." | Non-English pages |

## Verdicts for a review

- **Pass**: no breaches; small style notes only.
- **Changes needed**: one or more R4-R10 breaches, or voice problems an approver should fix.
- **Blocker**: any R1-R3 breach, or a factual claim that contradicts another Mosey Bank page.
